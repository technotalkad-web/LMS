### What KIVO sends

The released A.1 engine, `engine.a1.html` of the KIVO release build (outside this repository; read only):

- A feedback slide has four response modes, `likert-na` (default), `yes-no`, `free-text`, `stars`; it is never scored (`:7634-7647`). A multi-item slide becomes one synthetic slide per item with id `${slide.id}_${itemId}` (`:7925-7938`). After submit the widgets are disabled (`:7905-7909`): one response per item per attempt.
- A non-scored response is sent with verb `http://adlnet.gov/expapi/verbs/responded`, never `answered` (K-15, `:12772-12775`; the contract line `:12810`: "feedback item: likert | choice | long-fill-in (verb responded, no success), key none").
- The decode (`:13064-13097`): `likert` and `likert-na` → `interactionType: 'likert'`, `scale` = five ids `likert_0..likert_4` with the item's labels (defaults: `likert-na` = Not Applicable, Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Agree, Strongly Agree; `likert` = Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree), `response = 'likert_<index>'`; `stars` → `interactionType: 'likert'`, `scale` = `star_1..star_n` with descriptions "1".."n", `response = 'star_<n>'`; `yes-no` → `interactionType: 'choice'`, `choices` = `yes` and `no` with the item's labels, `response = 'yes' | 'no'`; `free-text` → `interactionType: 'long-fill-in'`, `response` = the text. An item left blank sends no `result.response` and `result.extensions["https://ambak.com/xapi/ext/question/unanswered"] = true`.
- The statement (`:13106-13143`): `object.id = ${activityId}/${slide.id}`, `definition.name` = the slide title, `definition.description` = the prompt (both language maps keyed by the package's declared language, `:12823-12829`), `definition.type = cmi.interaction`, `interactionType`, `scale` or `choices` when present, no `correctResponsesPattern`, no `result.success`; `result.response` as a string; `result.extensions[raw-response]` = the engine's native value; `context.registration` = the attempt id.
- The lifecycle harness answers each mode (`tests/bot/lifecycle/56-kivo-release-a-cmi5.spec.ts:929-936`) and asserts the shape (`:1464-1471`: verb responded, no `success`, no key). In a revision visit the harness asserts zero `answered` or `responded` statements, stored and on the wire (`:530-532`).

Older KIVO builds and third-party tools may send other `interactionType` values with `responded`, or none. They are handled, not assumed (section C).

### Terminology

- Feedback statement: an `xapi_statements` row whose `verb` is the `responded` IRI (the bare word `responded` is admitted too, as 0031 admits `answered`).
- Eligible attempt: a `course_attempts` row on one of the course's versions with `is_revision = false`, `is_preview = false` and `status` not `abandoned`. Under ADR 0014 these are the learner's official attempt, in progress or complete, and any granted retake.
- Question: one `interaction_id` (the statement's `object.id`) within one course.
- Counted response: a learner's newest feedback statement for a question on the course (`is_latest`).

### Scope

In: the Learner Feedback section on `/{org}/library/{courseId}/reports`, its CSV route, migration 0106, tests, documents. Out: the Learning Path reports page and the organisation Reports page (no feedback there; the latter is open to analysts), the Report Card and every manager surface, the CRM and Yoddha feeds, the tenant LRS feed (it already forwards raw statements and is unchanged), the LMS's own `course_ratings` widget and tile, SCORM 1.2 feedback (`cmi.interactions` are not unpacked; the page says so, as the per-question section does), the KIVO editor, any backfill or rewrite of stored statements, the question catalogue of P5.

## Decision

### A. The gate

1. Learner feedback, names and comments included, is shown to `canManage` roles only: super_owner and admin. Data Analysts see neither the section nor the CSV. Managers, who are `user`-role members, see nothing. This is the owner's rule read as code: "Admins only" = `canManage` (`lib/auth/permissions.ts:4-6`).
2. The gate runs on the server, twice for the page and once for the route. The page keeps its `canManage` redirect (`reports/page.tsx:66-69`). The section component takes the role and returns null unless `canManage(role)`, so a later widening of the page to analysts cannot leak the section. The CSV route answers 403 unless `canManage(role)`, after `requireOrgAccess`.
3. Every read of feedback rows happens after the gate, with the service client, and is pinned to a course id that the caller's RLS client has just confirmed belongs to the caller's organisation (the page's existing `courses` read, `:76-83`; the route's, as `learners/export/route.ts:54-63`). The new view is granted to `service_role` only, so no browser session can read it through PostgREST whatever its role.
4. No feedback row, name or comment is ever sent to the browser except as the rendered section an administrator asked for, or the CSV an administrator downloaded. No client component holds the rows.

### B. Source, eligibility and grouping

1. Source: `xapi_statements` rows with `verb in ('http://adlnet.gov/expapi/verbs/responded', 'responded')` and an `object.id`, joined to their attempt, version, package, profile and member row by the view `v_course_feedback_responses` (migration 0106, section D). The eligibility rule lives in that view and nowhere else, so the section, the summary function and the CSV always agree.
2. Eligible attempts: `is_revision = false`, `is_preview = false`, `status is distinct from 'abandoned'`, on the course's versions. What each excluded class contributes: a revision visit contributes nothing (the write route already stores only its session bookends, R-5, `route.ts:216-227`; the view's filter is belt and braces); a staff preview contributes nothing (a staff member answering a feedback slide is not learner feedback; `is_preview` is set at launch with `ALDS_P4` on and backfilled at 0100); a reset attempt contributes nothing. This goes further than ADR 0004 decision 9, which only forbids showing a reset attempt as "In progress": the learner course page shows a reset row as a neutral pill (ADR 0004 step 10), this page counts reset learners under Not started (`:271-275`), and the per-question breakdown on the same page counts statements from preview and reset attempts alike (`0031:350-357`; revision rows have stored no `answered` statement since R-5), so until a later record aligns it the two sections count different populations (decision 13). An in-progress official attempt contributes: feedback slides are usually answered before the course ends.
3. Counted response: a learner with two eligible attempts on the course (an official attempt and a granted retake, ADR 0014) may answer the same slide twice. The figures count the learner's newest response per question (`is_latest`, computed in the view over `(course_id, user_id, interaction_id)` by `stored desc, id desc`). The CSV lists every eligible row and carries `Counted = yes | no`. The page shows "N responses from M learners" when the two differ. If the learner's newest statement for a question is an unanswered one, that statement is the counted response and lands in "No answer"; the earlier answer is in the CSV with `Counted = no`.
4. Grouping: by `interaction_id` within the course, across every version of the course. Because the AU id is per package (Context), this groups across the versions of one language slot and not across slots: the same question in the Hindi slot and the English slot appears as two questions, each labelled with its slot language when the course has more than one slot. Response ids are language-independent (`likert_i`, `star_n`, `yes`, `no`), so if two slots ever share an AU id their responses group correctly. The question text and the scale or choice labels shown are those of the newest eligible statement for the question, in the package's declared language. The CSV carries the version id and the slot language on every row. A slide renumbered between versions appears as a new question, the same limitation the per-question breakdown has today; P5's question catalogue (ADR 0005) is the fix, not this record.

### C. The figures

Per question, from `course_feedback_summary` (section D), rendered in the page's own form (the `BigStat` cards and the table of the per-question section):

1. Stars (`interactionType = likert`, every scale id matches `^star_\d+$`): "Average rating" = the mean of n over counted responses, one decimal, with the count of ratings and the distribution per star. This is the owner's "average ratings".
2. Likert (`interactionType = likert`, scale ids `likert_*` or any other): the distribution per scale position in scale order, each with its label and count and share of counted responses. No mean: on the default scale `likert_0` is "Not Applicable", labels may be authored in any language, and a mean over a scale with an N/A position would mislead. This is the owner's "Agree/Disagree distribution". Every scale position is rendered in scale order; a position with no counted response shows 0 (the summary emits only buckets that have rows).
3. Yes/No (`interactionType = choice`): count and share per choice, labels from `choices`. Any `choice` statement with other ids is shown the same way.
4. Written comments (`interactionType = long-fill-in` or `fill-in`): the number of counted comments and the newest 50 of them across all comment questions (`is_latest` rows only, so the list matches the counted figure; a learner's earlier comment on a retaken course stays in the CSV with `Counted = no`), each with the learner's name (first and last from `profiles`, falling back to the email), the question, the date and the text. The text is rendered as a React text node inside a `<p>` with `whitespace-pre-wrap break-words`; `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` is forbidden in this component; a display cap of 1,000 characters with an ellipsis; the full text is in the CSV. "Showing the newest 50 of N. The CSV has all."
5. No answer: a statement without `result.response` (the unanswered extension) is a bucket "No answer" per question, shown and excluded from every average and share.
6. Other: any other `interactionType`, or none, under `responded` is shown as a distribution of raw response values with the label "Other", so a third-party package's feedback is visible and never crashes the page. Labels resolve with `readLang` (`lib/interactions/extract-xapi.ts:58-69`) over `description`, then `name`, then the id.
7. Freshness: the section is live (one function call per page load), unlike the nightly matview sections. The page header gains "learner feedback is live".
8. Empty and error states: no eligible row → "No learner feedback yet. This section fills when learners answer the feedback slides of a cmi5 or xAPI module. SCORM 1.2 packages do not send xAPI statements." A database without 0106 → one line "Learner feedback is available after migration 0106 is applied." Any other read error → "Learner feedback could not be read." with one JSON log line `{ event: "feedback_read_failed", course_id, code }` and nothing else.

### D. Database: migration 0106

The file is written in full beside this record as `feedback-migration.sql`; it becomes `supabase/migrations/0106_learner_feedback.sql`. It follows 0103's form: a header with order, independence, idempotency, locks and grants; a prerequisite DO block; Q-checks before; A-checks after; one transaction with `lock_timeout = '5s'`; `notify pgrst` at the end. It requires 0100 (`is_preview`) and 0103 (`is_revision`) and refuses otherwise; it is independent of 0105 (ADR 0020); nothing earlier must be avoided after it. Row writes: none.

1. Index `xapi_statements_responded_attempt_idx on xapi_statements (attempt_id) where verb in (…responded…)`. Why: the per-course read goes course → versions → attempts → statements. `xapi_statements_attempt_idx` alone would touch every statement of every attempt of the course (30 to 100 per attempt) to keep the few feedback rows; `xapi_statements_verb_idx` alone would touch every course's feedback rows in the shared database. The partial index holds only feedback rows, keyed by attempt, and its predicate is the view's verb filter byte for byte. Why in the transaction: production's table is empty and staging's is small, so one scan under `lock_timeout = '5s'` is quick; the file's header says that above 1,000,000 rows (Q1) the index is pasted alone first as `create index concurrently` outside a transaction, the 0100 §7 preference for this table (`0100:472-480`), and the file then finds it present. A5 also reads `pg_index.indisvalid`: a failed concurrent build leaves an invalid index of the same name, which `create index if not exists` would then skip; the header says to drop it concurrently and repeat the build.
2. View `v_course_feedback_responses` (`security_invoker = on`): one row per eligible feedback statement with the course, version, package and slot language, the learner's `user_id`, `email`, `first_name`, `last_name` and `employee_id`, `stored`, the engine's `timestamp`, `interaction_id`, `interaction_type`, `question` (description), `question_name`, `scale`, `choices`, `response`, `unanswered` and `is_latest`. Grants: `revoke all from public, anon, authenticated; grant select to service_role`. A view has no RLS; the grant is the whole gate, and the only readers sit behind `canManage` (section A). The profile and member joins are in the view so the CSV route reads names in the same pages as the rows and never pays one request per 150 learners (the 50-subrequest ceiling). `scripts/check-migration-grants.mjs` finds the grant.
3. Function `course_feedback_summary(p_course_id uuid)`, `language sql stable security invoker`: one row per (question, bucket) with `learners` (counted responses in the bucket), `responses` (every eligible row of the question), `first_at`, `last_at`, the newest statement's `question`, `question_name`, `scale`, `choices`, and `slot_languages`. The bucket for a written comment (`long-fill-in` or `fill-in`) is the marker `__text__`, so the summary carries no comment text from KIVO's types. For a third-party `responded` item of any other type every distinct raw response is its own bucket, so free text can reach the summary and a wordy item can fill the 1,000-row page; the reader flags a full page as `capped` and the page says the list is cut off. Grants: `revoke all from public, anon, authenticated; grant execute to service_role`. Its output is bounded by questions × (buckets + 1) for KIVO's types, far below PostgREST's 1,000-row cap; the reader orders by `(interaction_id, response)`.
4. Checks: Q0 objects present; Q1 table size (the index path); Q2 responded statements by interaction type and without a response; Q3 responded statements by attempt class (preview, revision, reset, eligible); Q4 (staging) the attempt of course `89891b95…` with its class. A1 the view's row count against Q3's eligible count; A2 (staging) the summary for that course: when Q4's class is `eligible` the rows' `learners` sum to Q4's `responded`, otherwise no row; A3 the privilege matrix in 0103's A5 form (expected no row: `service_role` has select on the view and execute on the function and nothing else); A4 `security_invoker` kept; A5 the partial index present, partial and valid (`indisvalid`); A6 the plan names an attempt index, never a sequential scan of `xapi_statements`.
5. Rehearsal (`scripts/migration-rehearsal/`). `assert-0106.sql` in the `assert-0103.sql` form: one transaction, `begin … rollback`, every check raises `assert-0106: …`. It inserts, on `seed.sql`'s rows, `responded` statements of the four A.1 shapes on attempt f3 (learner a1, completed on version e3 of course c2: eligible), one on f6 (a3, abandoned: excluded), one on a row it adds with `is_preview = true` (excluded) and one on a row it adds with `is_revision = true` and `status = 'in_progress'` (excluded; no other `in_progress` row for that learner and version, 0062). ## Consequences

- Administrators get a live Learner Feedback section per course and a CSV with names and comments; Data Analysts and managers get nothing, by the owner's rule.
- The database gains one partial index, one service-role-only view and one service-role-only function; no table, no RLS policy change, no row write, no matview, no refresh job.
- The eligibility rule for feedback is in one SQL object; every surface agrees by construction.
- The Performance tile's "Overall rating" (`course_ratings`) and the new "Average rating" (KIVO star items) are different measures and are labelled so; the section's sub-line says "from the feedback slides of KIVO modules".
- The tenant LRS feed already carries `responded` statements under the existing profile and is unchanged.
- The Workers budget: one RPC and one select per Reports page load; the export bounded at 20 pages.
- The view names its columns, so a later migration that adds columns to any of its tables (P5's 0101 and 0102 on `xapi_statements`) leaves it intact. A later migration that drops or retypes a column the view names (`course_attempts.is_preview`, `is_revision`, `status`, `profiles.first_name`, `course_packages.language`, and the rest listed in its DO block) must `drop view` and recreate it in the same file, and must restate its grants.
- Names come from the view's `profiles` and `organization_members` joins, not from `resolveEmails`; `lib/users/emails.ts` is unchanged and keeps its other callers.
- `tests/unit/csv-formula.test.ts` lists the route as the fourth CSV writer; its two rules (direct import, one escaped join) hold for it as for the other three.
- Open for later: a Learning Path feedback view; cross-slot grouping through P5's question catalogue; an organisation-wide feedback page for administrators; a date filter on the page's figures.

## Alternatives considered

- Read the rows through PostgREST embedded filters as `labels.ts` does and aggregate in the page. Rejected: thousands of rows per page load, over the 50-subrequest and 10 ms limits; no migration saved once names are needed.
- A materialized view refreshed nightly with the other report views. Rejected: another stale surface (staging has no scheduled refresh; the owner met this on 2026-11-07), a day's delay for feedback, and `refresh_report_views` would change; the live function is cheap at the expected sizes.
- No index. Possible: the attempt index serves the read. Rejected: it touches every statement of every attempt of the course on each page load, for no gain; the partial index costs one scan of an empty production table.
- A mean for likert items. Rejected: the default `likert-na` scale has "Not Applicable" at position 0 and labels may be in any language; the distribution is what the owner asked for.
- Group across language slots by the slide suffix of the object id. Rejected: slide ids across translated packages are not known to be stable; a wrong merge would be worse than two rows.
- Names through `resolveEmails` and a profiles lookup in chunks of 150 (the task's suggestion). Rejected for the CSV: a large export would need dozens of chunked requests on top of its pages; the view joins `profiles` and `organization_members` once in SQL, read by `service_role` behind `canManage`. The page's comment list could use either; it reads from the view for one rule.
- Count every statement (the per-question breakdown's rule). Rejected: a granted retake would count a learner twice in an average; the CSV keeps every row with `Counted`.
- Include reset attempts. Rejected: a reset attempt is never official and never in progress (ADR 0004 decision 9; blueprint R6), and its answers would stand as a learner's feedback on a module the learner must take again. Other surfaces do show a reset row (the neutral pill, "whose attempts were reset") and the per-question breakdown counts its statements; this record changes none of them.
- Build the CSV in the browser with `CsvButton`. Rejected: it needs the rows in the page payload.
- Stream the CSV. Not needed at 20,000 rows; recorded as a later option together with a date filter on the page.
