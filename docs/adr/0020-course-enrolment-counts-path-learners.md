## Goal and scope

Goal: the Library course card, the course Reports page and the two course report matviews count a learner as enrolled when any assignment reaches them, directly or through a Learning Path, once per (course, learner), from one definition in SQL. A path learner counts from the step's release date (the owner's option A), and a learner whom an enforced path still holds back does not count, so "enrolled" matches "can open the course today". A one-course read must stay cheap whatever the organisation's groups.

Out of scope: Learner Feedback (its own ADR); the Continue button (its own ADR); the staging report refresh (pg_cron on staging, the owner's paste, Round 80); Organisation Reports, which keeps counting members and official pairs and must not move; the CRM feed's contract; journey days and `content_scopes`, neither of which is an assignment; `org_public` visibility as a grant (it is read here only as a hold-back); any change to attempts, scoring, the launch page or learner pages.

## Decision

### A. The rule

1. A learner is enrolled on a course when both hold: (a) an assignment reaches them, that is a released direct row of any kind (`user`, `team`, `org`, `group`), or a released step of an active Learning Path they are assigned to (`user`, `team`, `org` or `group`); and (b) no enforced path holds the course back. (a) is `course-access.ts:137, 194`; (b) is `launch/page.tsx:655-668`. The pair is counted once. The hold-back copies the launch page exactly: every path holding the course is read, active or not; `org_public` paths hold back learners who were never assigned to them; the admin bypass is not copied (an admin assigned as a learner counts while held back). A direct row with a future `release_at` does not count until its date (today no reader applies the column to direct rows; owner decision 7). Inactive paths grant no access and do not count as a grant. `org_public` visibility, journey days and `content_scopes` are not assignments and do not count (unchanged).

2. One definition, in SQL: `v_course_enrollments_expanded` keeps its two columns (`course_id`, `user_id`) and `security_invoker`, and its body becomes rule 1. The two course matviews pick it up at their next refresh with no edit. `loadEnrolledCourseStatus` reads the view for the course, so the page's live numbers and the matviews share one definition and cannot drift. `now()` in the view is fine: the matviews snapshot it at their scheduled refresh and the pages read it live. Binding on the launch page: a change to its hold-back gate (`:655-668`) changes the view's `not exists` clause in the same pull request.

## Alternatives considered

- Expand paths and groups in TypeScript inside `loadEnrolledCourseStatus` and leave the view alone. The matviews and the page would still disagree, and the fourth copy of the rule would be the one most likely to drift. Rejected.
- Count a path learner from the day the path is assigned (option B). The course would show learners who cannot open it as Not started. The owner chose A.
- Apply option A as the first gate only (`course-access.ts`) and ignore the launch page's hold-back. The view would count learners who cannot open the course, which is what A was chosen to avoid; the independent check showed the case on a fixture. Rejected in favour of copying the launch page.
- Exclude dynamic groups from the view and resolve them only in TypeScript. Vertical and department assignments are dynamic groups, so the view would miss exactly the assignments the owner's organisation uses, and the pages and matviews would drift again. Rejected in favour of the SQL twin with a CI guard and a staging comparison.
- Join the whole group view into the enrolment views (the first draft). The group view is a `union` over every active group times every active member with a non-inlinable function call per key per pair, and a join cannot pass the group id into it, so every one-course read, the Library list and every refresh computed every group in the organisation (13 s per course and 37 s per refresh on the check's data). Rejected in favour of `group_member_ids` called per assigned group through `cross join lateral`.
- A SQL function that resolves one learner's groups (the `resolveUserGroupIds` shape) instead of a per-group function. The enrolment views start from assignments, so a per-group function is the shape they need; the per-learner shape would re-evaluate every rule per learner. Not chosen.
- Change the CRM feed to gate path steps by release and apply the hold-back. A contract change under `docs/CRM_INTEGRATION.md`, outside this record (decision 2).
- Recreate `mv_learner_metrics` to drop its redundant path branch and align it with the view. A matview drop and recreate inside the 15-minute gamification refresh window, for a small reporting gain (decision 5).
