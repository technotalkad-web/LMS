## Decision

### A. The rule

1. A learner's first completed attempt on a module is their official attempt (unchanged). Every later launch without a consumed grant is a **revision visit**: it runs on a **revision attempt row** (`course_attempts.is_revision = true`, written at launch by the service client), which never derives completion, success, score, `completed_at` or progress, stores only the session bookends, awards no completion XP, no activity XP, no streak day, no journey day and no "Most active" credit, fires no webhook, moves no "last active" or "last access" value, and never enters any attempt count, scoring window or SQL result; the row is re-seeded from the official attempt on every launch, so what the learner sees is the official attempt's progress, answers and result, which no revisit can touch; Ambak counts each visit in `revision_visits`. A new official attempt opens only by consuming an approved grant, in one database transaction, fresh and unseeded; the previous attempt stays as history and is shown as such; the scoring window and the `block` option are retired. Nothing in this record deletes, rewrites or backfills a learner row. **An official attempt that has received no AU-level `completed`, `passed` or `failed` is not complete, whatever it has received about the module's sub-activities** (any of the three completes it, `process-statement.ts:88–89, 101–104`): an assessment attempt reaches Ambak as a run summary about the assessment slide, stored with its verb (`statements/route.ts:74–85`) and never derived from (`process-statement.ts:91–99`), so an official course attempt in which assessment attempts have been taken but no course result reached is in progress with no score (`process-statement.ts:176–187`); it uses no slot in the window (`policy.ts:224–225, 293`), its next launch resumes the same row and registration (step 3; `launch/page.tsx:739–752, 1076–1086`) with the assessment attempts the author allowed and the learner has not used (Ambak neither counts nor withdraws them: Terminology), it is never a revision visit, no grant can open over it (step 11 (2) and (3); `consume_grant` `retake_live`), and the completion webhook does nothing for it (ADR 0015 Context, "the official course attempt left with assessment attempts remaining"). This is true today for every package; under decision 1 of the course-result analysis (the owner's "R1 yes", item 8 of their review request of 22 October, pending their approval sitting; KIVO ADR 0010's course-result step) an A.1 cmi5 package whose author set a counted assessment to more than one attempt (Maximum attempts 2 or more, or 0 = unlimited) sends its course result only when it is final, so the state lasts across launches until the learner passes, uses every assessment attempt the author allowed, can no longer reach the pass mark or presses "Finish now".

## Consequences

- **What learners see.** Their first run through a module is the official attempt. On a course exported with the A.1 engine, a revisit opens every slide and shows the official answers, assessment responses, progress and Results as recorded; nothing can be answered, retaken, started or restarted; Results, badge and certificate follow Ambak's recorded result. **Until re-export, and on KIVO SCORM, KIVO xAPI and some third-party courses, revisits still allow unrecorded re-answering**: a course published as SCORM or as KIVO xAPI, and any course not yet re-exported with the new engine, still lets the learner answer again, retake an assessment and Start Over on a revisit, and can show a new score, a pass, a badge and a certificate for that run; Ambak records none of it and the official result is unaffected (fixing those screens is a later engine release); if course-result decisions 1 and 3 are answered yes with S-A or S-B, the assessment and the game stay locked after Results on a KIVO SCORM course too, while a Start Over still re-opens the exercises (KIVO ADR 0010 "Limits and residuals"). Nothing done during a revisit is ever recorded as learning. Ambak keeps the visit itself: its `revision_visits` row, and on the revision row only the session's `initialized` and `terminated`, the row's `last_activity_at` stamp and any resume data the course saves there (a State document a pre-A.1 or third-party AU PUTs, the SCORM `cmi_data`; step 4). The next revisit overwrites that data, and no score, count or report reads it. A revisit earns no activity points, streak day or "Most active" credit and moves no last-active value; a learner who only revisits can show as inactive to managers and in Analytics, with "Last revisit" beside the flag. A learner who passed may request another official attempt and is warned that its result replaces the pass even if lower. A revisit's own reading position is reset to the official attempt's last slide at every launch; every slide is open. An official course attempt in which an assessment attempt has been taken but no result reached (on an A.1 cmi5 course whose author set a counted assessment to more than one attempt, under course-result decision 1) is neither complete nor a revisit: the next launch resumes it with the assessment attempts the author allowed and the learner has not used; the learner reads "in progress" with no score everywhere; reminders continue (`app/api/cron/reminders/route.ts:18–23` skips only completed or passed learners); after the due date the Attention Center counts them overdue (`0087_attention_overdue_count.sql:45–52` clears only a completed or passed attempt); a journey day that needs the module is not credited until the result is final (`0089` lines 42–44); an admin can reset the attempt but cannot end it with a result; and a language switch no longer resets it (step 18, if taken).

## Alternatives considered

- **A Review session inside the official registration** (cmi5's own model) instead of a flagged revision row: rejected; the AU's State PUT would raise the official row's progress (`state/route.ts` line 100 → `progress.ts` line 161), statements would stamp the official row (`process-statement.ts` line 191), and a `passed` could follow the official `failed` in one registration. No row at all: the same gating problem for SCORM.
- **The revision marker elsewhere**: a `cmi_data` key (forgeable through the commit body, `commit/route.ts` line 171); computing at write time (flips with `max_scored_attempts`, `policy.ts` line 248); `launch_mode = 'Review'` (P4-only, conflates Browse, and reading it under grading is what ADR 0005 note (j) forbids). A persisted column written only by the service client is immune to all three.
- **Counting visits** from tokens plus rows (SCORM under-counts), from `learning_sessions` (cmi5, P4 only) or a counter column (no timestamps): the table gives every format, timestamps and the dedupe.
- **What a revision row stores**: rejecting the three cmi5-defined outcome verbs with 403 (blueprint G3) would reject the exit array that carries `terminated` for pre-A.1 packages; store-and-exclude (blueprint G5 Q6 and G6) needs the matview rebuild and every statement reader filtered in the same release; "200, not stored" is the A.1 answer and is recorded as a departure (step 16).
- **Points for revisits**: suppressing only the `daily_activity` insert leaves the streak and `last_active_day` moving on every call; skipping the RPC is the only way to award nothing. Keeping the 2 October rule is reversed by the owner's point.
- **SCORM revisits**: serving nothing instead of `review`/`no-credit`: the standard's own revisit signal costs nothing and the commit gate does not depend on it.
- **Forced restart and retakes**: excluding retakes from the selection would leave a retake on a replaced package with no reset and no new grant (a live retake blocks a grant); today's behaviour loses the authorisation silently. A `decision_note` prefix instead of `reissued_from`: three readers would parse notes.
- **The history**: a snapshot column at grant time is derivable from the chain and adds no information; leaving it to the attempt list, which admins can hide from learners, would not meet "clear to the learner".
- **The scoring window (Q1)**: keeping it as authorisation in advance needs the basis restricted to `best` (`first` would score attempts that can never count; `latest` and `nth` can lower a pass), an explicit learner choice beside "Revise", and revision from the first completion; about three more executor-days and a weaker reading of the policy's points 1 and 2; Block goes either way.
- **Cutover for unflagged rows**: closing them (an abandon per row, an LRS `abandoned`, a "reset" count) or classifying at write time (per-request scoring reads in every route) over flagging in place at launch.
- **Reads that feed the verdict**: fail open, as today; a guessed verdict can flag an official row or open an unflagged revision row, and both are sticky.
- **Review-only revision in the LMS alone**: the LMS cannot lock a slide; the lock is the engine's (KIVO ADR 0010); the LMS's part is to ignore whatever the content does on a flagged row (R-5), which holds for every package type.
- **Grants after a pass (Q2)**: the option (c) as published (learners cannot ask; admins grant with confirmation) needs an admin "grant to this learner" action that does not exist and is neither included nor priced, so it would behave as "no re-certification path at all"; an admin-initiated per-learner grant to a passed learner and a time-based re-enrolment window are recorded later options.
- **Where a revisit's Results takes the verdict**: a recomputation of the seeded bookmark under the current package (it can disagree with the record after a package update) or a `contextTemplate` extension of `LMS.LaunchData` (a conformant AU would echo the verdict into its statements): the separate LMS-written document.
